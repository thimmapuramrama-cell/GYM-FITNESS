CHOOSE YOUR
FITNESS STYLE

TRADITIONAL

TRADITIONAL COMMERCIAL GYMS

These are classic membership-based gyms offering a wide range of equipment, including cardio machines, free weights, resistance machines, and group classes like Zumba or HIIT. They often include amenities such as locker rooms, showers, saunas, and sometimes pools. Best for a broad audience, from beginners to intermediate fitness enthusiasts, and thrive on high membership volume.

BIG BOX

BIG BOX GYMS

Large-scale gyms with extensive facilities, often part of a chain. They provide diverse equipment, group classes, and amenities like swimming pools and saunas. They appeal to both beginners and seasoned fitness enthusiasts and usually offer more affordable memberships due to scale.

BOUTIQUE

BOUTIQUE FITNESS STUDIOS

Smaller, specialized gyms focusing on a single type of workout, such as yoga, barre, or cycling. They emphasize community, personalized attention, and unique experiences, often charging higher rates per class or via class packs.

24/7 ACCESS

24/7 FITNESS CLUBS

Open 24 hours with key-card access. Perfect for shift workers and busy lifestyles. Secure, monitored, and flexible with no time restrictions for workouts.

CROSSFIT BOXES

CrossFit Boxes

CrossFit gyms, often called “boxes,” focus on high-intensity functional training combining weightlifting, cardio, and bodyweight exercises. They foster a strong community culture and group-based workouts. Equipment typically includes barbells, kettlebells, pull-up bars, ropes, and rowing machines. CrossFit gyms are suitable for those seeking strength, endurance, and a competitive yet supportive environment.
